# What happened to the weirdest prediction markets of 2026: pre-registration

Written 2026-11-03. Changes after this date get a dated note and stay visible. The piece publishes in January 2027 as a new
page, distinct from `/weird-prediction-markets-report/`, which analyses the curated board.

**Population.** Every market that appeared on a daily prediction-market board in `data/boards/YYYY-MM-DD.json`, from the first
board (2026-04-25) to the last of 2026, at the first price we printed. On 2026-11-03 that is 832 distinct tickers from 162
boards (530 Polymarket, 292 Kalshi, 10 Polymarket US), 385 of them past their close time. Sports and combo boards are out.

**Outcome.** The exchange's own resolution, pulled from its API by ticker (Kalshi) or condition id (Polymarket). Unresolved,
voided, or unmatched markets are counted and reported. They are never scored as losses.

**Primary measure.** What $1 on every Yes at the first printed price returned, across all resolved markets, then by tier
and by venue. The board picks entertaining longshots, so a loss here is the likely result and the piece reports it plainly.
The price is the ask we printed; fees and fills are not modelled, and the piece says so.

**Secondary.** Hit rate against the printed implied probability, in five bins. Ten named stories, chosen after the numbers are
computed, each with a linked market and resolution rule.

**What the piece may not claim.** This is the record of a board chosen for comedy. It says nothing about prediction markets in
general and gives no betting advice. Quips stay out of the numbers.
